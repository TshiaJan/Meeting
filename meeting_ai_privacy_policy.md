# Privacy Policy for Meeting AI

**Last updated:** September 20, 2026

Meeting AI ("we", "our", or "the app") provides an intelligent meeting assistant, real-time transcription, audio summarization, and task extraction tool. We recognize that business discussions, strategic plans, and team meetings contain sensitive confidential information. Protecting your privacy and intellectual property is foundational to everything we build.

---

## 1. Information We Collect and Process

### a. Meeting Audio, Transcripts & Notes
- **Transcripts & Summaries:** Meeting notes, agendas, transcriptions, speaker designations, and action items generated within Meeting AI are stored locally on your device by default.
- **Local Cache:** Recordings and meeting archives remain under your control on your local device unless you choose to export or sync them.
- **No Training on Private Conversations:** We do not use your private enterprise discussions, audio recordings, or transcripts to train public foundational AI models.

### b. AI Processing & Transcription
- Audio transcription and summarization are performed ephemerally using secure automated language pipelines. The processed data is returned to your device and not retained on external processing servers.

### c. Anonymous Diagnostics
- We collect standard, non-personally identifiable diagnostic information (such as crash logs, audio codec performance metrics, and OS versions) through Google Play services to ensure microphone compatibility and app stability across Android hardware.

---

## 2. In-App Purchases and Billing

Meeting AI offers optional Pro and Team subscriptions. Financial transactions and payment details are processed exclusively and securely through the **Google Play Store Billing API**. We do not collect, view, or retain your credit card or financial information.

---

## 3. Data Retention and User Ownership

- **Full Data Ownership:** You own 100% of your notes, transcripts, audio recordings, and extracted action items.
- **Immediate Data Deletion:** You can delete individual meetings, action item histories, or all app data at any time directly within the app or via Android device settings.
- **Zero Third-Party Data Sharing:** We do not sell, rent, or trade your meeting transcripts or business notes to advertisers, recruiters, or data brokers.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

Meeting AI is designed for working professionals and general business audiences, and does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 13 years of age.

---

## 5. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically revise this policy to reflect new capabilities or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted here and within the app with an updated revision date.
